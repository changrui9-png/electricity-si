The Microgrid Dilemma

Why Traditional Microgrids Burn Too Much Diesel

Rule-based PLCs lack predictive foresight. They over-spin generators, cycle engines unnecessarily, and curtail clean solar when batteries experience temporary load shifts.

01

Costly Genset Short-Cycling

Conventional controllers fire up diesel generators every time load momentarily spikes, causing catastrophic wet stacking, carbon build-up, and tripled maintenance overhauls.

02

Solar Inversion & Curtailment

Without foresight into upcoming industrial shifts or weather fronts, energy managers throttle solar arrays to preserve spinning reserves, throwing away free kWh.

03

Premature Battery Degradation

Aggressive micro-cycling without state-of-health (SoH) awareness degrades lithium cells rapidly, destroying CAPEX returns before the project hits year four.
